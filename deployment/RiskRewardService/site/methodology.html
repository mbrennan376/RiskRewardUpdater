Methodology

HOW THE NUMBERS WORK

Allocation follows logarithmic price position

Each chart has a lower line and an upper line. The lower line maps to a 10% allocation of invested cash and the upper line maps to 0%. Prices between them are interpolated on a logarithmic scale, matching the logarithmic price axis used by the charts.

Suggested allocationclamp(10 × ln(upper ÷ current) ÷ ln(upper ÷ lower), 0, 10)

On a logarithmic scale, halfway means the geometric midpoint—not the ordinary arithmetic midpoint. Therefore, when current = √(upper × lower), the displayed allocation is 5%.

Risk/reward position

The position shown along the colored range is the inverse view of the same calculation:

Position from lower to upperclamp(100 × ln(current ÷ lower) ÷ ln(upper ÷ lower), 0, 100)
  • 0% position: at or below the lower line; allocation is 10%.
  • 50% position: logarithmically halfway; allocation is 5%.
  • 100% position: at or above the upper line; allocation is 0%.

Relationship to Mark Gomes’s methodology

Mark Gomes describes using his fundamental risk/reward charts to buy nearer the green/lower line, take profits nearer the red/upper line, and size positions using his “10-point rule.” The exact equations above are this site’s implementation of that guidance; the cited article does not publish this formula.

Original source: “Get Rich On Stocks! (The Rules),” Mark Gomes Research, October 11, 2019

The calculation is descriptive, not investment advice. Market-alert rules and company-specific fundamental considerations can override mechanical position sizing in Mark’s methodology.
