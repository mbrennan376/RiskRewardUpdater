ABOUT THIS SITE

Risk/reward charts with current context

This site organizes screenshots from Mark Gomes’s public weekly research broadcasts and combines them with current market prices to show where each price sits between the chart’s lower and upper risk/reward lines.

The chart images and line values are reviewed manually. Price quotes are supplied by third-party market-data services and may be delayed, unavailable, or denominated in the currency shown beside the quote.

Your investment amount stays in your browser

You can enter your total invested cash in the Suggested allocation tile. The site multiplies that amount by the suggested allocation percentage to show a personal target investment amount. Use one consistent portfolio currency; the resulting target is expressed in that same currency.

All user-entered data for this feature is stored only in this browser’s local storage. It is never transmitted to the website, uploaded, or stored in the site’s files or servers. Each browser and device has its own saved value. Selecting Clear—or clearing this site’s browser data—removes it.
