ABOUT THIS SITE

Risk/reward charts with current context

This site organizes screenshots from Mark Gomes’s public weekly research broadcasts and combines them with current market prices to show where each price sits between the chart’s lower and upper risk/reward lines.

The chart images and line values are reviewed manually. Price quotes are supplied by third-party market-data services and may be delayed, unavailable, or denominated in the currency shown beside the quote.

Your portfolio stays in your browser

Use Set holding or Update holding on a selected stock to enter total invested cash, choose USD or CAD as the portfolio currency, select a 1–10 point alert threshold, and enter shares held with an allocation multiplier from 0.0× to 2.0×. For cross-listed Canadian companies, choose the exact US or Canadian symbol where you hold the shares. The chart list shows how many allocation-scale points the holding is above or below target, in green when it is within the selected threshold and red when it is outside.

For a nonzero holding with complete quote data, its chart also shows total holding value and today’s dollar and percentage gain or loss. The header shows the combined daily dollar change only when at least one nonzero holding has been entered and every entered holding has all required quote and conversion values.

When the held listing uses the other currency, the latest dated USD/CAD rate published with the market prices translates the chart quote into the selected listing, sizes the share target in that listing, and converts holding values into the portfolio currency. Cross-currency target calculations and holding summaries are paused if that rate is unavailable or stale.

All user-entered portfolio data is stored only in this browser’s local storage. It is never transmitted to the website, uploaded, or stored by the site’s files or servers. Each browser and device has its own values. Use Export JSON to save a local backup—including the selected currency, point threshold, and held symbols—and Import JSON to restore or move all saved settings.
